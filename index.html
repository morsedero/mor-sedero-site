Mor designs interactive game audio that connects gameplay, technology, and emotion - from impactful combat and creature sound design to adaptive music and responsive audio systems built in FMOD, Unreal, and Unity.

With a background as a multi-instrumentalist, composer, and sound designer, he approaches audio from both creative and technical perspectives - creating experiences that support gameplay, strengthen storytelling, and react naturally to the player's actions.

Mor mentors aspiring game audio designers and performs with the band BOBBA. He continues exploring sound through guitar, drums, keys, bass, banjo, bouzouki, and sitar — always looking for new ways to shape musical ideas into unique experiences.

SERVICES

What Mor brings to a project.

Audio is built into the game from the start using Unreal Engine, Unity, and FMOD. From adaptive music and gameplay states to parameter-driven events, spatial audio, and scalable audio pipelines, every system is designed to be responsive, maintainable, and easy to expand as development evolves.

From weapons and creatures to UI, environments, and cinematic moments, every sound is designed to support gameplay as much as atmosphere. Studio recordings, field recordings, synthesis, and layered processing are combined to create distinctive assets built for interactive experiences rather than linear playback.

Every score is composed, produced, and mixed from the ground up, with music written to reinforce pacing, gameplay, and storytelling. Genres range from orchestral and hybrid scoring to electronic, rock, ambient, and world music, matched to whatever the project calls for. Whether cinematic or adaptive, the focus is always on creating music that feels connected to the player's experience rather than simply playing in the background.

Music, dialogue, and sound effects are balanced for clarity, impact, and consistency across games, trailers, film, and promotional content. Every mix is prepared to communicate effectively whether heard through headphones, mobile speakers, or full-range monitoring systems.
