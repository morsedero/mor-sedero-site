Daisey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08. Daisey is a day-planning app at morsedero.com/daisey/now, made by Mor Sedero.

What Daisey accesses

  • Your Google account: your email address and your Google Calendar (to read and add events for your plan).
  • Your Trello account (if you connect it): your boards and cards, to read and update tasks.

How it is used

Only to show and schedule your own tasks and calendar inside Daisey. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, shared with third parties, or used to train AI models.

What is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on our hosting provider (Netlify) so you stay signed in. Your tasks and calendar data stay in your own Google and Trello accounts; Daisey does not keep a separate copy beyond what the app needs to run.

Google API data

Daisey's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored tokens deleted, email morsedero@gmail.com.
